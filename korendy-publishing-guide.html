How to publish a blog post

This guide walks you through writing a blog post with an AI tool and publishing it in Shopify. The AI writes the post content and its colours; you paste them into the right places and fill in the post's settings.

The AI guide

content-guide-ai.md tells the AI tool exactly how to write a post. Always give it to the AI together with your request.

Shopify access

To Online Store → Blog posts
Turkish admin: Online Mağaza → Blog gönderileri

4 steps

Prepare a brief, ask the AI, create the post in Shopify, then check it before publishing.

01 / Brief

Prepare your brief

Collect these before asking the AI

WhatExample
TopicNiacinamide nedir, cilde ne yapar?
Main keyword What people search forniacinamide serum
Target readerYağlı ve karma ciltler, K-beauty'ye yeni başlayanlar
Products to feature Links/products/…, /products/…
Collection to promote Link, optional/collections/niacinamide
Primary colour Optional#2f7d5b. Leave empty for Korendy pink
Anything elseTone, length, things to mention or avoid

Choosing a primary colour

  • Pick one colour that fits the post's topic, for example green for a green-tea post or orange for a ginseng post.
  • The AI builds the soft backgrounds and borders from it.
  • Mid or dark colours work best; very light colours are darkened automatically so text stays readable.

02 / Ask the AI

Ask the AI to write the post

Claude, ChatGPT or another AI tool

  1. Open your AI tool.
  2. Attach or paste the AI guide.
  3. Paste your brief. For example:
    Using the attached guide, write a blog post. Topic: … · Main keyword: … · Target reader: … · Products: … · Collection: … · Primary colour: …
  4. The AI answers with three files:
    post-body.html

    The post content.

    post-colors.css

    The post's colours.

    post-meta.txt

    The title and the description.

  5. Read the text before publishing. Check product names, claims and links. Ask the AI to change anything that isn't right.

03 / Shopify

Create the post in Shopify

Go to Online Store → Blog posts → Add blog post, or open an existing post.

Title

Copy the Title line from post-meta.txt into the Title field. This is the page's main heading, and the same text is used as the SEO title below. Don't add the title to the content.

Content: paste post-body.html here

  1. In the Content editor, click the <> (Show HTML) button.
  2. Delete anything in the box and paste the whole post-body.html.
  3. Click <> again to see the result.

Avoid editing these posts in the visual editor: it can change the HTML. Make changes in the <> view, or ask the AI.

Excerpt

Copy the Description line from post-meta.txt into the Excerpt field (Turkish: Alıntı). It's 1–2 sentences (about 140–160 characters) that sum up the post and make people want to read it; the same text is used as the meta description below.

The excerpt appears under the title on the post page and on the blog cards. Don't copy the first paragraph word for word.

Featured image

Upload the post's main image in Featured image.

  • Size. A landscape image at least 1600px wide works best.
  • Alt text. Describe what's in the picture.
  • Don't repeat it. The image is shown at the top of the post automatically, so it shouldn't appear in the content.

Post fields

In the Metafields section at the bottom of the post page:

FieldWhat to do
YazarPick the author. Their name, photo and bio appear on the post.
KoleksiyonPick a collection to show a product slider inside the post. Leave it empty if the content already has product cards or a collection promo, so products don't appear twice.
Post colorsPaste the whole post-colors.css. Leave it empty to use the standard Korendy pink.

Search engine listing (SEO)

Scroll down to Search engine listing and click Edit (Turkish: Arama motoru listesi → Düzenle):

  1. Page title: paste the Title line from post-meta.txt.
  2. Meta description: paste the Description line from post-meta.txt.
  3. URL handle: don't change it. Leave it as Shopify creates it from the title.

Blog, tags and publishing

  • Blog. Choose the right blog, for example K-BLOG, Rutinler or Cilt Dengesi.
  • Tags. Add 2–5 relevant tags if your team uses them.
  • Visibility. Set it to visible, or schedule a date. Save, then preview the post.

04 / Checklist

Check before publishing

Go through each item before the post goes live

  • The title is set and not repeated in the content
  • The content was pasted in the <> view; headings, product cards and the closing block look right
  • Title (40–60 characters, no brand name) in both the Title field and the SEO page title
  • Featured image added, with alt text, and not repeated in the content
  • Description (140–160 characters) in both the Excerpt field and the meta description
  • Yazar picked; Koleksiyon left empty if the post has its own product cards
  • Post colors pasted, or left empty for pink
  • Product links open the right products
  • Checked on a phone: product cards, buttons and images
