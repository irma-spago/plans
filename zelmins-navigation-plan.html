FlyRank / Zelmin’s navigation recommendations

A clearer menu and footer for zelmins.com

We recommend replacing the hidden desktop menu with a simple, visible menu of five items, and turning the footer into four clear columns. Your key pages become easier to find for visitors and for Google, and your products get a proper shopping page that can rank in search.

5 itemsVisible main menu1 new collectionBreath Care Essentials6 addressesCleaner web addresses4 columnsOrganised footer
What this plan covers
  • Desktop and mobile menu
  • Breath Care collection
  • Menu labels
  • Web addresses and redirects
  • Footer structure
  • Every footer link

The main menu gets five items: Shop, Our Flavors, The Science, Reviews and Journal. Shop leads to a new Breath Care collection with your three core products. Everything else moves to the footer.

Biggest changes
  • The menu becomes visible on desktop. Today, even on large screens, visitors have to open a hamburger icon to see any page.
  • Shop gets a real page. Routine Builders becomes Breath Care Essentials, the page the Shop menu item links to.
  • Clearer labels: “Did You Know?” becomes Journal, “See Reviews” becomes Reviews, and Subscriptions becomes Subscribe & Save.
  • Cleaner web addresses without the ® symbol, each with an automatic redirect so no links or rankings are lost.
  • The footer gets four labelled columns (Brand, Shop, Company, Learn) and a slim bar for the legal links.

What we found

5 issues
  • The menu is hidden on desktop.Even on large screens, visitors have to open a hamburger icon to see any pages.
  • “Shop Now” has no page of its own.It only opens a list, so there’s no shopping page for Google to rank.
  • Your main product isn’t in a dedicated collection.The closest one, Routine Builders, already holds the three core products but isn’t linked as the main shop page.
  • Some labels are unclear.“Did You Know?” and “See Reviews” don’t describe their pages well.
  • Some menu links point to pages that can’t rank in Google,or to products that are being removed.

Key changes

A new Breath Care collection

We’ll turn Routine Builders into Breath Care Essentials, which contains Minty Mouth®, Bedtime Batch and the Tongue Scraper. The page will include a short introduction, a “build your routine” section and a few FAQs. It becomes the page the Shop menu item links to.

Subscriptions

The subscription stays, listed as Subscribe & Save in the Shop menu. Existing subscribers will also find a Manage Subscription link under Account and in the footer.

Starter Pack removalLater phase

We’ll remove the Starter Pack from the site and send its visitors to the Minty Mouth® page. Because the Starter Pack is still used in sections across the website, this step waits until those sections are updated.

Footer / 05

What we found

6 issues
  • All links sit in one unlabelled list.Reviews, Apparel, About Us, Contact and the policy pages are mixed together, so the footer is hard to scan.
  • No products in the footer.Minty Mouth®, Bedtime Batch and the Tongue Scraper aren’t linked, and neither are Our Flavors, The Science or the Journal.
  • The Shipping Policy isn’t linked anywhere.The page exists, but visitors can’t find it from the footer.
  • The text-us number can’t be tapped.“Text us 424.354.1184” is plain text, so mobile visitors have to copy it by hand.
  • No logo.The brand column has a tagline and social icons but no logo.
  • Small technical issues.The Instagram link uses an old, insecure address (http instead of https). The affiliate link points to the non-www version of the site, which forces a redirect. Labels use inconsistent capitalisation (“Terms Of Service”, “Become An Affiliate”). The “Powered by Shopify” link has no text that screen readers can read.

Footer / 08

Mobile & smaller fixes

Mobile
  • The Brand column stays open at the top.
  • Shop, Company and Learn become tap-to-open sections, so the footer doesn’t turn into a long scroll.
  • All links stay in the page code even when a section is closed, so Google can still find them.
Smaller fixes we’ll include
  • Fix the Instagram and affiliate links so they go straight to the right address.
  • Use consistent capitalisation in all labels.
  • Give the “Powered by Shopify” link readable text, or remove it.
  • Mark the footer as a navigation area for screen readers.
