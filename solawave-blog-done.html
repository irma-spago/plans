01 / Templates

Resources page and template cleanup

Done

One article template, one blog template, a new Resources page, and author pages built on the same system

11 removed

The old FlyRank article, blog and author sections, their templates, and the scripts only they used.

8 added

Shared content styles, one post card and one author card, plus the Resources, Authors index and Author page sections and templates.

7 updated

Article and blog pages rebuilt, templates pointed to the new sections, duplicate structured data removed.

What's new

  • Article page. Hero with author and published/updated dates, table of contents, share links, collection promo, author box, related posts, and structured data.
  • Blog page. Search, a featured post, post cards with excerpt and author, pagination, and structured data.
  • Resources page. Every blog and article in one place, with a tab per blog, so Google finds all content and internal linking gets stronger.
  • Authors index and author pages. All authors, each author's latest posts across every blog, and links between authors.
  • One codebase. main-article and main-blog are now the only article and blog templates. Every content page shares the same post card, author card and styles.
  • Cleaner structured data. Blog and breadcrumb markup now prints once instead of twice.

02 / Product promos

One product promo format

Done

Inline-CSS product cards replaced with topic-matched collection promos

  • Old cards removed. The Solawave team and previous agency product cards built with inline CSS are gone from every article.
  • Collection promo on every article. Each piece now promotes the collection that fits its topic, built into the template and matching the brand.
  • New Collection field on blog posts. Anyone publishing content picks the relevant collection from a dropdown. No custom code needed.

03 / Images

Replace unapproved images

Done

Only real, approved Solawave devices in content

  • Unapproved images replaced. AI-generated images of devices that don't exist are swapped for approved Solawave images.

Coming next

  • General images. We'll regenerate the general images that don't feature light therapy devices, using images with models.

04 / Authors

Consistent authors

Done

Same authors across all content, on one set of author pages

  • Authors assigned. Every content piece now uses the same authors from /pages/authors.
  • New author box and pages. The author box, authors index and author page are part of the new templates.

Once the new templates are live

  • Remove duplicate reviewer pages and redirect them to the matching author pages.
  • Remove the second Author field so everyone who writes content uses one field, consistently.

05 / Optimizations

Meta titles and descriptions

Done

Every content piece has a meta title and description that fits in search results

  • Optimized. Meta titles and descriptions rewritten across all content so they no longer get truncated.
  • Missing ones added. Pages without a meta description now have one.

Coming next

  • Excerpts. We'll update article excerpts, which now show on post cards across the blog, Resources and author pages.
  • Copy. We'll improve thin copy, starting with pages that perform well but have room to grow.
