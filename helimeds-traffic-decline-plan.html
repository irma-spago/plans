04 / Causes

Possible causes

Ranked from most to least likely

  1. November 2026 core update. The drop starts Nov 10, the update's start date. All four topics fell 38–58%, and core updates hit health and medication content hardest.
  2. Unapproved peptide content. 12 of the 54 are peptide guides (sermorelin, Semax, BPC-157, KPV, TB-500, epitalon). Dosing advice for unapproved substances from a site that sells treatments faces a high trust bar.
  3. Thin sourcing. Several pages cite 1–3 outside sources, including how-to-take-blue-chew, can-i-take-nad-and-nac-together, how-often-can-you-take-tadalafil-5-mg and should-nad-be-taken-on-an-empty-stomach.
  4. Outdated pages. 6 pages are still dated 2024: wegovy-coupons, wegovy-patient-assistance, zepbound-side-effects, guide-to-ozempic-injection-sites, semaglutide-dosage and trulicity-dosing. wegovy-coupons still quotes Wegovy at about $1,430 a month.
  5. Official and big medical sites win. Brand, coupon and side-effect queries (Wegovy, Ozempic, Zepbound, BlueChew) compete with manufacturer sites, drug labels and large publishers.
  6. Weak date signals. 21 of 54 posts show Oct 15, 2026 as their publish date with no separate update date, and 146 blog URLs share one sitemap lastmod (Oct 21). Google may ignore these freshness signals.

05 / Next steps

What happens next

Recommended once the update settles

  • Wait for the rollout to finish (about two weeks) before large changes.
  • Strengthen the peptide guides with clinical citations and clearer evidence limits.
  • Add sources to the pages with 1–3 outside citations.
  • Refresh the 6 pages dated 2024, starting with wegovy-coupons and wegovy-patient-assistance.
  • Fix date signals. Show real "last updated" dates and set sitemap lastmod only when a page changes.
  • Fix the 3 slug typos with 301 redirects.
